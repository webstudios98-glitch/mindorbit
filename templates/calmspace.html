🕊 CalmSpace — Overcome Anxiety & Stress

When your mind feels heavy, pause for a moment here. You are safe, and you have power to breathe through it 🌌

Follow this simple 4-7-8 breathing technique:

  • Inhale deeply for 4 seconds 🌬️
  • Hold your breath for 7 seconds 🫁
  • Exhale slowly for 8 seconds 🌊

1️⃣ Grounding Technique (5-4-3-2-1)

When anxiety strikes, look around and identify:

  • 5 things you can see 👀
  • 4 things you can touch ✋
  • 3 things you can hear 👂
  • 2 things you can smell 👃
  • 1 thing you can taste 👅

2️⃣ Release Tension

Slowly tense and release each muscle group in your body — start from your toes, move to your legs, stomach, shoulders, and finally your face. Feel the relaxation spread.

3️⃣ Reframe Your Thoughts

Write down your worry, then challenge it. Ask — “Is this really true?” or “Is there another way to see this?” Most anxieties lose power when faced with truth.

✨ You are enough, just as you are.
